# Employee Security Policy (Synthetic)

> Entirely synthetic document for platform testing. Not a real policy.

## Access control

All employees authenticate with single sign-on and hardware MFA. Access to
production systems requires membership in an approved access group, reviewed
quarterly.

## Data handling

Confidential documents are stored only in approved, encrypted repositories.
Data at rest must use AES-256 encryption; data in transit must use TLS 1.2
or higher.

## Retention

Business records are retained for 7 years unless a legal hold extends the
period. Ephemeral chat messages are retained for 90 days.
